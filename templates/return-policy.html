1. 7-Day Exchange & Return Eligibility

We want you to wear our handcrafted creations with supreme confidence. You may request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving your parcel under the following simple guidelines:

  • The garment must be unworn, unwashed, and undamaged with original tags intact.
  • Items should be returned in original protective packaging whenever possible.
  • In case of a size exchange, our master tailors can either adjust your existing piece or stitch a freshly sized garment for you.

2. Made-to-Measure & Custom Sizing Guarantee

Because each custom-stitched piece is individually handcrafted to your unique measurements (bust, waist, hip, height, sleeve length):

  • Complimentary Alterations: If the fit needs fine-tuning, our atelier provides 100% free master tailoring alterations.
  • Simply message our master concierge on WhatsApp at +91 93504 39667 with photos showing the fit adjustments needed.

3. Simple 3-Step Return & Exchange Process

1

Notify the Atelier

Click our direct WhatsApp button below or message 93504 39667 with your Order # and preference (size swap or return).

2

Doorstep Pickup

Our courier partners (Bluedart, Delhivery, Xpressbees) collect the packaged garment from your address at no friction.

3

Exchange or Refund

Upon brief quality check at our studio, your replacement garment is dispatched or your refund is initiated immediately.

4. Refund Timelines & Payment Reversal

Once your returned piece arrives at our atelier and passes our brief quality verification:

  • UPI / Prepaid Orders: Refunds are credited directly to your UPI ID or original bank account within 24 to 48 hours.
  • COD Advance Orders: Security advances (₹99) are credited back to your preferred UPI handle or bank details shared with concierge.
  • Store Credit Option: You may also choose 100% store credit with a bonus 5% artisan gift voucher valid forever.

5. Order Modifications & Pre-Dispatch Cancellations

You may modify sizing, shipping address, or cancel an order within 12 hours of placing it, before our artisans begin cutting the fabric. To modify, immediately reach out via WhatsApp at +91 93504 39667.
